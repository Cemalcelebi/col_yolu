## Key Question 1: Is there physical, third-party evidence that construction/energization is progressing on the announced schedules?

### Takeaway
Only DGXX Columbiana has a dense third-party paper trail (building permit, air permit, city incentive vote) consistent with an active build, but nothing independent confirms Phase 1 (15 MW) will be energized by 15 Dec 2026. AIB CLT1 has county-level process activity (development agreement at second reading 3 Nov 2026) but no permit/contractor evidence; QMLS Atlanta is colocation (no construction evidence expected or found); VVPR Mo i Rana has only company-announced local contractors, no Norwegian third-party record found.

- VVPR: 12 Oct 2026 "mobilization" is company-reported; no Norwegian third-party corroboration found; the absence of an M&E EPC award and of a definitive tenant as of 3 Nov 2026 means the $411M build is not yet contracted.
